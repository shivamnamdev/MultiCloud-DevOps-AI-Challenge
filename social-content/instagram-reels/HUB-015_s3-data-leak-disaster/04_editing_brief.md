## ⏱️ 1. Pacing & Assembly Guidelines
- [ ] **Silence Stripping (Speed Run):** All dead pauses, breath gaps, and hesitation silences cut tighter than 0.3 seconds.
- [ ] **J-Cuts & L-Cuts:** Audio transitions smoothly before or after visual cuts to maintain fluid conversation momentum.
- [ ] **Visual Shift Rule:** Visual state changes on screen every 3 to 6 seconds (camera crop zoom, B-roll overlay, diagram highlight, or kinetic typography).
- [ ] **No Monotonous Wide Shots:** Alternating between Wide (100%) and Punch-In (115%) on key emphasis words.

---

## 🔊 2. Psychological Sound Design (POH Heartbeat Formula)
*Sound design manages heart rate and emotional tension.*

- **Hook Riser (0:00 – 0:03):** Fast whoosh or reverse cymbal leading into the visual title drop.
- **Tension Risers:** Gradual pitch-elevating synth riser mimicking human heartbeat before major reveals.
- **The Tension Drop:** Abrupt 0.5-second total audio silence right before dropping the core solution or punchline.
- **Tactile UI SFX:** Pop, click, paper slide, and keyboard tap cues layered under every diagram or text appearance.
- **Background Music Ducking:** Music ducks automatically by -18dB to -24dB whenever voiceover is active.

---

## 🎨 3. Graphics, Motion & Typography
- **Font Choice:** Modern sans-serif (e.g. Outfit, Montserrat, or Inter Bold).
- **Text Pacing:** Kinetic 1–3 word pop-ups synced to speech cadence (no long paragraphs on screen).
- **Color Accent:** High-contrast color highlight (Electric Yellow or Bright Mint Green) on power words.
- **Diagram Cleanliness:** Vector assets with subtle drop shadow or glow.
