## 🎙️ 1. Audio Pre-Flight Check (The Acoustic Invariant)
*Audio is 80% of retention. Echo destroys viewer trust immediately.*
- [ ] **The Clap Test:** Clapped hands in the center of the recording room—zero flutter echo or hollow ring detected.
- [ ] **Acoustic Dampening:** Heavy curtains drawn, rugs positioned, soft pillows/blankets placed out-of-frame to absorb reflections.
- [ ] **Microphone Placement (Inverse Square Law):** Lavalier mic positioned 6 inches from mouth (not clipped to chest where clothes rustle).
- [ ] **Gain Staging:** Audio levels peak between -12dB and -6dB (never peaking in red).
- [ ] **Room Tone:** Recorded 15 seconds of clean room silence for AI noise profile calibration.

---

## 📱 2. Camera & Lighting Rig
- [ ] **Lens Cleaning:** Physically wiped phone/camera lens with microfiber cloth.
- [ ] **4K Resolution:** Camera locked at 4K @ 24fps or 30fps.
- [ ] **Manual Lock:** Exposure and White Balance locked (no auto-exposure pulsation or color shifts).
- [ ] **Eye-Level Framing:** Camera positioned exactly at eye level (Rule of Thirds grid enabled).
- [ ] **Key Light:** 45-degree diffused window light or softbox illuminating face.
- [ ] **Practical Backlight:** Warm desk lamp / accent light placed behind creator for separation from background.
- [ ] **Distance from Wall:** Creator seated 3–6 feet away from background wall to create natural depth of field.

---

## ⚡ 3. Performance & Screen Presence (The +30% Rule)
- [ ] **Lens Connection:** Stared directly into the black glass of the camera lens (never at the screen or phone display).
- [ ] **Energy Multiplier:** Speaking with intentional 130% physical and vocal energy (compensating for the 30% energy lens absorption).
- [ ] **Dynamic Pauses:** Deliberately paused before key punchlines to allow natural jump cuts without word clipping.

---

## 🎬 4. Shotlist & B-Roll Capture
- [ ] **A-Roll (Primary Talking Head):** Full script delivered in tight, energetic sections.
- [ ] **B-Roll 1 (Terminal / Code Screen):** Screen recording of terminal commands, architecture diagram, or code demo.
- [ ] **B-Roll 2 (Physical Props / Gestures):** Close-up shot of hands holding physical analogy props or sketching on paper.
- [ ] **B-Roll 3 (Emotional Cutaways):** Reaction shots, micro-expressions, or head-tilt contemplation.
